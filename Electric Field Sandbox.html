Physics Behind the Simulation

Coulomb's Law:
F = k |q₁q₂| / r²

Electric Field:
E = F / q

Electric Field of a Point Charge:
E = kq / r²

Superposition Principle:
Etotal = E₁ + E₂ + E₃ + ...

Red charges are positive and blue charges are negative. Electric field arrows point away from positive charges and toward negative charges. The simulation uses normalized charge units and a simplified visual scale; displayed force magnitudes are illustrative rather than SI-calibrated.
